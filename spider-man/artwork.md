# Spider-Man artwork

The 5 wallpapers are art inspired by Spider-Man, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Marvel, Sony and Insomniac Games. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-red-and-blue.jpg`: Spider-Man swinging over the city; artist unknown
- `02-city-dusk.jpg`: Spider-Man at dusk; artist unknown
- `03-absolute-peak.jpg`: Spider-Man at the absolute peak; artist unknown
- `04-darker-spider.jpg`: The darker spider; artist unknown
- `05-brand-new-day.jpg`: Spider-Man, Brand New Day (fan made); artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: night navy background, white text, Spider red accent, red, green, gold, web blue, violet and cyan.
